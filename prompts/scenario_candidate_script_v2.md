# SocialFlux Direct Candidate Narrative v2

Write one original, compact social-scenario dossier from the construction brief.
This dossier will be normalized into a benchmark candidate and later reviewed by
a human; it is not a trajectory or an answer. All people, organizations, and
events are fictional, and the task contains no real personal data. Follow the
brief's requested domain instead of defaulting to a workplace: the relationship
may be familial, communal, educational, professional, civic, creative, service,
or another socially plausible setting. Do not infer sensitive traits, diagnose
anyone, or provide technical operating or safety instructions.

Use 700–1,000 words. Include only:

1. the two principal characters and their relationship;
2. the concrete current conflict, deadline, and practical stakes;
3. three to five consequential prior events;
4. what each person publicly wants, privately fears, and uniquely knows;
5. at least three materially different but plausible choices;
6. one later verification/event node whose meaning depends on earlier conduct;
7. a short opening situation, without scripting the rest of the conversation.

Keep all names, organizations, facts, events, and wording original. Preserve the
brief's mechanism, power structure, information asymmetry, and temporal design.
Do not mention schemas, latent variables, initial numeric values, triggers, benchmark tasks,
scores, labels, prompts, or model behavior. Do not write a screenplay or long
dialogue. Do not prescribe a best strategy or ending.
The dossier may establish that a later verification procedure is scheduled,
but must not reveal its result as if the characters already know the future.

Return Markdown only, with concise section headings.
