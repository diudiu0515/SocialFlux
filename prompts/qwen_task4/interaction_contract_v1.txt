Qwen Task4 interaction interface, version 1.

Analyze the latest evaluated_agent action only. Use the existing output_schema
and event_status_pairs literally. Person names belong in prose; actor fields use
role IDs. owner is evaluated_agent, even when the represented action_owner is
environment_agent, collective, or unspecified. A claim uses status "reported",
not "firm". Firm/conditional statuses belong to commitments. Each event has one
assertion_mode, chosen from asserted, negated, hypothetical, quoted, definitional.

Record currently expressed requests, proposals and commitments faithfully.
Do not omit an event to avoid a validation error. Empty arrays are appropriate
only when the latest text has no corresponding event. A report of another
person's claim does not independently establish its truth. Keep uncertainty,
negation, quotation, intention and completion distinct.

Quote the latest action verbatim, including its original language; never translate
evidence spans. Resolve only supplied, active proposals by the other participant.
Use at most four supplied evidence turn IDs. Correct a rejected enum or span
without rewriting the semantic interpretation to satisfy an expected outcome.
Return one JSON object and no extra fields or reasoning text.
