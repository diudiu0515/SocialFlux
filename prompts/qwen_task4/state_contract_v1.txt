Qwen Task4 state interface, version 1.

Follow the existing evidence definitions and actor binding. The output_schema
below describes the JSON wire format, not a target state or a desired outcome.
Return all supplied state and dynamics paths exactly once. Do not write numeric
state updates. Python owns calibration and applies the update once.

For ordinary_new_evidence or decisive_new_evidence, direction is literally
"increase" or "decrease": the defined variable's intensity goes up or down.
It does not mean good/bad, positive/negative sentiment, or benefit/harm.
directness is literally "direct" or "indirect". evidence_span is a nonempty
verbatim substring of the latest action, never a translation or a history quote.
evidence_feature may be null; do not force a feature match to obtain an update.
For no_relevant_evidence and repeated_evidence_only, direction, directness and
evidence_feature are null and evidence_span is "". Explain each judgment briefly.

Preserve the scope and modality of evidence. Uncertainty about an outcome is
not proof that it cannot occur. Willingness to bear a risk is not abandonment of
the goal. A current refusal or decision may take effect now; a promise of future
performance does not establish that the promised action happened. Judge each
construct separately rather than propagating one inference to every variable.

On a validation retry, fix the named field and preserve the underlying evidence.
Do not erase supported judgments or change their direction merely to pass a
schema check. Check exact keys, enums, variable coverage and quote binding before
returning one JSON object. Do not include reasoning in the final JSON.
