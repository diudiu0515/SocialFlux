# SocialFlux Narrative Structure Extraction v1

Purpose: extract a reusable social-interaction structure from a film or television work, then define how it must be transformed into an original SocialFlux source. This is structural analysis, not adaptation or scene reproduction.

Identify:

- the abstract relationship and social mechanism;
- the power/resource asymmetry;
- each side's incompatible but understandable goal;
- information asymmetry and consequential prior events;
- why later interpretation depends on earlier interaction;
- where meaningful adaptation is possible and where no universal response exists.

Copyright and originality constraints:

- do not reproduce dialogue, scene text, character names, distinctive objects, fictional institutions, or plot sequence;
- do not summarize the complete work;
- separate the abstract mechanism from protected surface expression;
- require a new setting, new characters, new events, new stakes, and original language;
- record only the work title and medium as internal provenance;
- identify elements that must be discarded before release.

Quality constraints:

- avoid reducing every source to a generic argument;
- preserve the source's distinctive causal tension while changing its expression;
- ensure the resulting structure can sustain 5–10+ free-form turns;
- ensure T1/T2/T3/T4 suitability without prescribing actions or outcomes;
- internally check that the output contains no quotation or copied scene.

Return JSON only conforming to schemas/narrative_structure.schema.json.
